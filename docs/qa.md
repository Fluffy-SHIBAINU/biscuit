Run 1 (2026-10-27, by Claude): a desktop browser and the API against a local server with the auth bypass, one real call per model, the QA household deleted at step 12. Two failures fixed in 08376fd (Ana's "Next: the mailbox phone" did nothing; a read took 17.5 s against the 12-second budget because Muse Spark reasons by default). Another limit seen: while a phone's microphone prompt is pending, the voice check shows "Listening…" with no buttons; on a phone the prompt is modal, so it resolves either way. Runs 2 and 3 are the founder's, on the iPhone, the Android phone and a laptop: speaking, the camera at a real mailbox, the printed bill in the frame, iOS notifications and the 911 sheet are the parts no browser can stand in for.

Known limits to state rather than fix: no wake word (Talk, a tap or a notification starts Biscuit); the mailbox phone must stay on the page, plugged in; English only; one family member told.
