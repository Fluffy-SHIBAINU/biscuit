## What changes in the production

- **Narration.** The founder's voice carries the story; Biscuit speaks only his own lines. Fifteen VO lines above,
  about 110 seconds of speech. Recorded on a phone in a quiet room is enough; I align each line to its cut. Fallback: a
  Gemini narrator voice distinct from Biscuit's (Fenrir stays his).
- **Pace.** A cut every three to six seconds. Waiting time (the ten-second read, the page loads) is cut out, not sped
  up; a short "Reading…" beat stands in. No shot runs without a voice over it except the two one-second beats.
- **Motion.** Slow pushes on the phone at the moments that matter (the quote highlight, the rule, the Sent badge, the
  calendar dot); the phone and laptop slide in and out rather than cut; kinetic text for the numbers ($1,234.50, 150%
  FPL, 20 minutes) timed to the word.
- **Sound.** The chime and Biscuit's lines as recorded. Optional quiet music bed under the VO only (the founder's track,
  or none; no stock music without a licence).
- **Fixes carried over.** Recordings transcoded to constant 30 fps before composing (the flicker); every line pushed to
  start after the previous clip ends (the overlaps).
- **Optional.** Eight seconds of the founder on camera at "I'm Shawn" (a phone selfie clip, landscape); the card works
  without it.
